Handedness

Handedness & Lifespan Analysis

↗

A statistical analysis investigating the relationship between handedness and lifespan using historical handedness surveys, US mortality data, and Bayesian probability modeling.

Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib

What it does

  • Combined National Geographic handedness data with US death-distribution statistics across age groups
  • Modeled the probability of left- and right-handedness conditional on age and birth year
  • Applied Bayes' theorem to estimate age-at-death distributions for each handedness group
  • Compared modeled mean lifespan across handedness groups and recalculated results using different study years

Key findings

  • Historical left-handedness rates varied substantially across birth cohorts
  • The modeled lifespan difference varied with the assumed study year, highlighting the effect of demographic and cohort assumptions
  • Demonstrated how probabilistic modeling can uncover patterns from demographic data
